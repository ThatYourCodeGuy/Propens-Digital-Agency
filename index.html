Frequently asked questions

The questions prospective clients usually ask before signing on.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok, depending on where each brand's audience actually is. We don't put a brand on a platform just to say we cover it.

Do you create content, or just schedule it?

Both. We plan the calendar, develop the content ideas, write the captions, and handle scheduling and publishing — then track what's working and adjust.

What industries have you worked with?

Fintech, media and personal brands, advisory and consulting, and real estate so far — the process adapts to each industry's audience and tone rather than staying one-size-fits-all.

How do you measure success?

Audience growth, engagement, and visibility, tracked against the goals we agree with each client up front. We report on what actually moved, not vanity metrics.
